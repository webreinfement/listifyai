FAQ

Common questions

How does it work?

Install the Chrome extension, enter your license key, and open any Etsy or eBay listing editor. Describe your product in the chat and RankifyAI generates a fully optimized listing instantly.

Is this a subscription?

No — it's pay-as-you-go. You pay $8 or $15 and get 30 days of access. When it expires, you just pay again if you want to continue. No automatic charges.

What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter gives you 250 listing generations per day, Pro gives you 500. Both have the same AI quality and features.

Does it work on eBay?

Yes! RankifyAI works on both Etsy and eBay. Just tell the AI which platform you're listing on and it tailors the output accordingly.

How do I get my license key?

After payment, your license key is emailed to you instantly. Just paste it into the extension and you're live.
